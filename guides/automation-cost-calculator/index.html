        Where the Exact Pricing Crossover Happens

        The cost crossover between Make.com and n8n Cloud is controlled by average steps per workflow. At 2,500 monthly runs with simple 2-step or 3-step automations (such as catching a form submission and adding a CRM record), Make.com uses 5,000 to 7,500 credits. That fits comfortably inside Make.com Core's 10,000-credit floor at $9 to $12/month annual, beating n8n Cloud Starter (€20/month, roughly $22 USD) by nearly half.

        Once workflows expand to 8 to 20 steps (AI enrichment, webhook parsing, multi-table updates, branching, or looping through line items), per-module and per-task billing multiplies fast. A 2,500-run pipeline with 12 steps burns 30,000 Make credits ($27 to $36/month on Core) and 27,500 Zapier tasks (far past Zapier's $89/month 5,000-task plan), while n8n Cloud still counts 2,500 executions and stays on Starter at €20/month annual.

        At high run counts with shallow workflows (for example, 10,000 simple 2-step runs), Make.com consumes 20,000 credits ($18 to $24/month annual), whereas n8n Cloud requires Pro 10,000 executions (€50/month annual, roughly $54 USD). High-frequency, 2-to-4-step triggers favor Make.com; multi-step pipelines and AI agent loops favor n8n Cloud.

        Honest Limitations to Weigh Before Committing

        Make.com credit burn and regional pricing

        Polling triggers that check an inbox or API every 1 to 15 minutes consume a credit on every scheduled check, even when zero new records exist. Switching to instant webhooks avoids idle credit burn. Checkout pricing also varies by billing region: published Core 10k pricing starts at $9/month annual in several regions but displays at $11 to $12/month annual ($16 monthly) for US addresses. Auto-topup credit packs cost 25% more per credit than upgrading your base tier.

        n8n Cloud technical learning curve and Euro billing

        n8n expects basic comfort with JSON payloads, expressions, HTTP requests, and OAuth setup. Non-technical operations teams usually build their first workflows faster in Zapier or Make. n8n Cloud offers a 14-day free trial (1,000 executions) with no permanent free Cloud tier, bills in Euros (so USD card statements fluctuate with exchange rates), and keeps its Business tier (SSO, Git version control, multiple environments) self-hosted rather than on standard Cloud.

        Zapier connector breadth vs task escalation

        Zapier still has the largest catalog of plug-and-play SaaS integrations and the quickest setup for non-technical users. Built-in Filter, Formatter, Paths, and Looping steps cost 0 tasks, which reduces burn on data-cleanup workflows. Once monthly volume climbs past 2,000 to 5,000 action tasks, however, Zapier becomes significantly more expensive than both Make.com and n8n Cloud, and MCP tool calls consume 2 tasks each.
