Who Should Skip Each Platform

Transaction fees are only half the decision. Each platform enforces trade-offs around merchant-of-record taxes, automation depth, and branding.

Skip Gumroad if...

Your monthly direct sales consistently clear the break-even GMV shown above (often $600 to $1,600/mo depending on list size and ticket price). At $3,000/mo in direct sales, Gumroad's 10% flat cut plus processing costs roughly $360/mo, which easily exceeds an entire Kit Creator or beehiiv Lite subscription. Note that Gumroad acts as Merchant of Record for global VAT/sales tax, which Kit Commerce and Stripe do not handle automatically without extra tax configuration.

Skip Kit if...

Your list exceeds 25,000 subscribers and you do not need complex visual funnel branching. At 50,000 subscribers, Kit Creator steps up to the 55k bracket ($379/mo monthly), whereas beehiiv Lite sits at $299/mo monthly ($249/mo annual) with 0% platform commerce cut and a built-in Ad Network. Also skip Kit paid tiers if you only send simple broadcasts under 10,000 subscribers: Kit's Free Newsletter tier already covers up to 10k subscribers and Kit Commerce.

Skip beehiiv if...

You have under 2,500 subscribers, sell under $700/mo, and want branding removed cheaply. After beehiiv's October 2026 restructuring, Lite starts at $59/mo monthly ($49/mo annual) and keeps beehiiv footer branding. Removing branding requires Pro at $109/mo monthly ($95/mo annual), whereas Kit Creator removes branding at $39/mo monthly ($32.50/mo annual equivalent).

Don't forget Merchant of Record tax overhead

Gumroad's 10% fee includes global VAT, GST, and US state sales tax collection and remittance. If you move digital downloads to Kit Commerce or beehiiv + Stripe, check whether you need Stripe Tax or local VAT registration for EU/UK buyers before counting 100% of the fee spread as pure profit.

Methodology & Fee Assumptions

All fixed subscription brackets come directly from official public pricing pages checked in November 2026 (Kit, beehiiv, and Gumroad). Because payment processing varies by card network and country, the calculator uses three explicit assumptions:

  • Gumroad processing estimate: Modeled as 10% flat platform fee + $0.50 per transaction (labeled estimate for card/PayPal processing overhead). Actual processor charges vary by buyer currency and PayPal vs card checkout. Excludes Gumroad Discover's 30% marketplace fee.
  • Kit Commerce & annual rates: Kit Commerce charges 3.5% + $0.30 per sale across Free and paid plans. Kit's monthly Creator brackets ($39 / $59 / $89 / $139 / $199 / $379) are exact published figures; annual billing applies Kit's published 2-months-free discount (10× monthly billed annually, matching $390/yr on the 1k tier).
  • beehiiv + Stripe processing estimate: Paid beehiiv tiers take 0% platform cut on paid subscriptions and digital products. The calculator adds standard US Stripe card processing (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction) as a realistic apples-to-apples net take-home baseline. International cards or currency conversion add roughly 1% to 1.5% on Stripe.
  • Break-even GMV formula: Let P be average order price. Switching from Gumroad saves 0.065 + 0.20 / P per dollar of GMV on Kit and 0.071 + 0.20 / P per dollar of GMV on beehiiv. Dividing the monthly SaaS fee by that per-dollar savings yields the exact monthly GMV break-even point.
