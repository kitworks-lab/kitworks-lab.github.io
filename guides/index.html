Methodology, Data Sources, and Limitations

Every calculator in this directory runs directly in your browser using plain JavaScript and static pricing rules derived from official public vendor pricing pages checked in November 2026. No inputs are sent to a backend server, and no account is required to see results.

What these calculators measure

  • Published list prices (USD): Monthly and annual-equivalent rates taken directly from each vendor's public pricing page as of November 2026.
  • Contractual and feature gates: Hard tier boundaries that change your real cost, such as StreamYard restricting consumer plans to personal use, Restream capping Studio at 720p on Standard, or beehiiv keeping its footer branding until Pro.
  • Explicitly labeled estimates: Where payment processor fees (Stripe card rates, Gumroad processing) or network overhead (RTMP bitrate headroom) depend on buyer geography or ISP stability, the exact formula and assumption appear right next to the output.

What these calculators do not claim

  • Not fabricated lab reviews: We do not invent hands-on test scores, subjective star ratings, or fake customer quotes. These guides compare documented pricing math, tier limits, and policy terms.
  • Taxes and short-term promos excluded: Published USD rates exclude VAT, GST, local sales tax, regional JPY/EUR pricing adjustments, and introductory first-month promo coupons.
  • Prices change: SaaS vendors frequently revise seat minimums, subscriber brackets, and included credits. Always verify final checkout terms on the vendor's official pricing page before committing to an annual plan.
