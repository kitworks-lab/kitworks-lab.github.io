Who Should Skip Each Tool

No single multistreaming service fits every broadcast setup. The pricing cliffs above create clear cases where a tool costs more than it returns.

Skip Restream if...

You run a browser-based talk show at 1080p on a sub-$20 budget. Restream Standard ($19/mo monthly, $16/mo annual) keeps Restream Studio locked at 720p, forcing a jump to Professional ($49/$39). Also skip Restream if you expect bundled AI clip editing or long-term cloud VOD archival; recordings expire after 15 to 30 days and AI Clips cost extra.

Skip StreamYard if...

You broadcast on behalf of an LLC, agency, brand, or employer and want transparent self-serve pricing. StreamYard's Plan Usage Policy limits Essential, Core, and Advanced strictly to individual personal use. Solo streamers who want no watermark at 2 destinations should also note that Essential ($18.99/$14.99) still displays the StreamYard watermark until Core ($44.99/$35.99).

Skip Riverside if...

You stream primarily from OBS Studio with custom scenes, VTuber rigging, or browser-dock overlays and only need a passive RTMP cloud splitter. Riverside is built around its own browser studio, locks multistreaming behind Grow ($45/$34), and caps separate-track local recording downloads at 20 hours per month on Grow.

Skip OBS Multi-RTMP if...

Your ISP upload line lacks at least 25% headroom above combined stream bitrates, or you regularly host non-technical remote guests. Pushing three 6 Mbps streams locally requires 18 Mbps raw (22.5 Mbps safe headroom). Any upload congestion drops frames across all platforms at once.

Methodology & Bandwidth Assumptions

This calculator is built directly from official published pricing pages and policy documents verified in November 2026 (Restream, StreamYard, Riverside). It does not include fabricated hands-on benchmark scores or subjective latency ratings.

  • OBS Multi-RTMP upload formula: Raw upload equals destinations × bitrate (Mbps). Safe required upload applies a 1.25× headroom multiplier (25% buffer for audio streams, RTMP protocol overhead, chat traffic, and ISP jitter).
  • Cloud relay upload formula: A single upstream ingest equals 1 × bitrate × 1.25. Savings equal (destinations - 1) × bitrate raw Mbps removed from your home upload line. Note: if you push two separate encoded feeds (16:9 and 9:16) from OBS instead of cloud-cropping one feed, cloud ingest doubles to 2× bitrate.
  • Currency & promos: All numbers are published USD list prices excluding local taxes/VAT and temporary introductory discounts (such as StreamYard's occasional first-year promo banners).
  • Open-source math: Inspect the full pricing logic in app.js or on github.com/kitworks-lab.
